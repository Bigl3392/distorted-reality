# COVER ART SPECIFICATION (16:9)

**Module**: M13 Cover Image Asset  
**Specification**: `DOD.md` Section A.1 & `VIDEO_SPEC.md`

---

## 1. Specifications & Format
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 landscape (mandatory for LabLab / Vultr submission card).
- **Target Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 pixels (Full HD).
- **Format**: PNG or high-quality JPEG (< 5 MB).
- **Color Space**: sRGB.

---

## 2. Visual Design & Theme
- **Theme**: Dark instrumentation / cryptographic proof / security containment.
- **Palette**:
  - Background: Deep fortress dark `#0b0f19` and `#111726`.
  - Accents: Technical cyan `#06b6d4`, electric emerald `#10b981`, caution amber `#f59e0b`.
  - Contrast: High-contrast white and light slate typography.
  - Strict Darla Prohibition: Zero pastel pinks/purples, zero bakery textures, zero whimsical imagery.
- **Visual Elements**:
  - Central focal point: A clean cryptographic shield enclosing a container wireframe.
  - Supporting graphic: Subdued monospace terminal code showing illustrative hash-chained journal entries (illustrative sample: `seq: 042, head_sha256: 189f4003...`) and container receipts.
  - Subtle background topology: Stylized node graph connecting "CONTROL PLANE [C]" to "SANDBOX HOST [S]" with the label `--network none`.

---

## 3. Typography & Text Hierarchy
- **Title (Primary)**:
  `DISTORTED REALITY`
  - Font: Bold, geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter / JetBrains Sans), tracking tight.
  - Color: Pure white with subtle cyan rim lighting.
- **Subtitle (Secondary)**:
  `SANDBOXED AI WORKFLOW EXECUTION WITH AUDIT PROOF ON VULTR`
  - Font: Monospace uppercase, tracked.
  - Color: Electric cyan `#06b6d4`.
- **Badges (Lower Third)**:
  - `BLAST RADIUS ZERO · PATTERN A`
  - `VULTR SERVERLESS INFERENCE`
  - `SYNTHETIC WORKFLOW EVIDENCE`
- **Claim Discipline Compliance**:
  - Zero hype slogans: No "Guaranteed ROI", No "Will Transform Your Business".
  - Clean, factual technical identity.
